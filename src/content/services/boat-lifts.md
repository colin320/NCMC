## Types of boat lifts: elevator, 4-post and cradle

| Lift type | How it works | Good fit for |
|---|---|---|
| Elevator lift | A platform rides up and down a track mounted to the dock | Fixed docks with sturdy pilings |
| 4-post lift | Cables raise a cradle between four free-standing pilings | Larger boats and open water slips |
| Cradle / beamless lift | A cradle sits between pilings with no overhead beams | Boats with towers or T-tops, or owners who want a clean look |

## Why keep your boat on a lift

A boat left in salt water between trips picks up growth on the hull, and the metal parts below the waterline corrode faster. Up on a lift it stays dry and washdowns go quicker. It also isn't banging against the dock when the wind picks up or a big wake comes through.

## Adding a lift to an existing dock

A lift puts the whole weight of the boat on a few pilings. On an older dock we check those pilings and the framing before anything else. Sometimes they are fine as they are. Sometimes the lift needs its own pilings, and you will know that before the job is priced.
