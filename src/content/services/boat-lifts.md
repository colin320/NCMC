## What kinds of boat lifts are there?

| Lift type | How it works | Good fit for |
|---|---|---|
| Elevator lift | Platform travels up and down on a track mounted to the dock | Fixed docks with sturdy pilings |
| 4-post lift | Cradle raised by cables on four free-standing pilings | Larger boats, open water slips |
| Cradle / beamless lift | Cradle between pilings with no overhead beams | Clean look, boats with towers or T-tops |

## Why put your boat on a lift?

Keeping a boat out of the water between trips cuts down on marine growth, blistering and corrosion, and makes washdowns easier. It also keeps the hull off the dock during wind and wake.
