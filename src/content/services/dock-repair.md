## What are the signs a dock needs repair?

- Soft, cracked or loose deck boards
- Wobbling or leaning sections
- Pilings with heavy rot or marine borer damage at the waterline
- Rusted or missing bolts and hardware
- Railings that move when you lean on them

## What happens after a storm?

Stay off a dock that looks damaged until it is inspected. Take photos for insurance. Call us and we will inspect it, secure anything dangerous, and give you a written estimate for repair or rebuild.
