faqs:
  - q: What is a swinger lift?
    a: A swinger lift mounts to a dock or piling and swings the jet ski up and over onto the dock or out of the water using a winch or motor. It is a good fit for fixed docks.
  - q: What is a drive-on jet ski float?
    a: A drive-on float is a floating platform you ride the jet ski onto. It rises and falls with the tide and attaches to a floating or fixed dock. A recent project used a WaveArmor float.
  - q: How much weight does a PWC lift need to hold?
    a: Size the lift to the jet ski's dry weight plus fuel and gear, with a margin. A recent project used a 1,500 lb swinger lift. We confirm the right rating for your machine.
  - q: Can I add a jet ski lift to my existing dock?
    a: Usually. We check the dock structure where the lift mounts and reinforce if needed.

## Swinger lift or drive-on float?

| | Swinger lift | Drive-on float |
|---|---|---|
| Mounts to | Fixed dock or piling | Floating dock or fixed dock with float |
| Tide | Works at any tide | Rises and falls with the tide |
| Launch | Winch or motor lowers the PWC | Ride on, push off |
| Best for | Fixed piers | Floating dock setups, frequent riders |
