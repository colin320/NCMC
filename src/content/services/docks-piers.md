## What a dock on Topsail Sound or the ICW needs

A dock on Topsail Sound or the ICW has to reach water deep enough for the boat at low tide, and we check that first. The pilings have to match the soil and the current, and the hardware has to hold up in salt. Some owners want a long walkway out to a fishing platform. Others need a low landing so they can step onto the boat.

## Fixed dock vs. floating dock

A fixed dock stays at one height. That's the usual pier on the Intracoastal Waterway and Topsail Sound, and it's what a lift wants to hang from. A floating dock moves with the tide, so the step to the boat doesn't change. A lot of places use both, with a gangway between them. Our [floating docks](/services/floating-docks/) page covers that setup.

## How a dock project works

1. **Site visit.** We check the depth and the shoreline, look at the neighboring docks, and figure out how the equipment gets in.
2. **Estimate and permits.** You get a written price. We explain the CAMA and local permit steps before you sign.
3. **Build.** Pilings go in first. Framing and decking follow.
4. **Walk-through.** We walk the finished dock with you and talk about upkeep.
