## Who is a floating dock right for?

Floating docks are a good fit if you board from a low boat, launch kayaks or paddleboards, keep jet skis, or have family members who find ladders hard. Because the deck stays a fixed height above the water, the step down to the boat is the same at every tide.

## How is a floating dock built?

A floating dock is a frame and deck on top of sealed floats. It is held in place by guide pilings with hoops or rollers, so it can move up and down but not drift. A hinged gangway connects it to the fixed pier and changes angle as the tide moves.

## What should I ask before buying one?

- How tall are the guide pilings relative to storm surge?
- What is the gangway slope at low tide?
- What float and frame materials are used?
- Who handles the permit?
