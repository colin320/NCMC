## Who a floating dock suits

A floating dock suits a low boat or a kayak launch. The deck stays a set height above the water, so the step down doesn't change with the tide. It also helps anyone who has trouble with a ladder.

## How a floating dock is built

The dock is a frame and deck on sealed floats. Guide pilings, with hoops or rollers, keep it from drifting while it moves up and down. A hinged gangway runs from the fixed pier down to the float. The angle changes as the tide comes and goes.

## Questions worth asking first

- How tall are the guide pilings compared with storm surge?
- How steep is the gangway at low tide?
- What are the floats and the frame made of?
- Who files the permit?
