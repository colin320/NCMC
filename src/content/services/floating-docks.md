## Who a floating dock suits

A floating dock makes the most sense if you board a low boat, launch kayaks or paddleboards, or keep jet skis. It also helps family members who have trouble with ladders. The deck stays at a set height above the water, so the step down to the boat doesn't change with the tide.

## How a floating dock is built

The dock is a frame and deck on top of sealed floats. Guide pilings with hoops or rollers keep it in place, so it moves up and down without drifting. A hinged gangway runs from the fixed pier down to the float and changes angle as the tide comes and goes.

## Questions to ask before you buy one

- How tall are the guide pilings compared to storm surge?
- How steep is the gangway at low tide?
- What are the floats and frame made of?
- Who handles the permit?
