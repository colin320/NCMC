## Signs a dock piling needs replacing

Look at the waterline first. Heavy wear there is the usual sign, along with rot at the top, borer holes, and a piling that leans. So is a dock that moves when people walk on it. The deck can look fine and still be in trouble if the posts aren't.

## Pilings for lifts and new docks

On a new [dock or pier](/services/docks-piers/) we lay the pilings out before we drive them. A [boat lift](/services/boat-lifts/) on an older dock often needs its own. The posts already there were never asked to hold a boat in the air.
