## Signs a dock piling needs replacing

Look at the waterline first. Heavy wear there is the most common sign, along with rot at the top, borer damage, and pilings that lean. So is a dock that moves when people walk on it. Everything above the water depends on the pilings, so a problem down there eventually shows up everywhere else.

## Pilings for lifts and new docks

New [docks and piers](/services/docks-piers/) are laid out piling by piling on the plans. A [boat lift](/services/boat-lifts/) added to an older dock often needs its own pilings, because the dock's pilings were never sized for that load.
