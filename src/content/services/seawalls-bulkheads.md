## How a vinyl bulkhead holds the shoreline

Interlocking vinyl sheets are driven into the ground along the shoreline to form the wall. A cap and horizontal wales stiffen the top. Tieback anchors buried in the yard hold the wall against the pressure of the soil and water behind it, and filter fabric lets water drain out while keeping the soil in.

## Vinyl vs. wood bulkheads

| | Vinyl sheet piling | Wood |
|---|---|---|
| Rot and marine borers | Not affected | Can be affected over time |
| Up-front cost | Usually higher | Usually lower |
| Maintenance | Low | Higher |
| Look | Clean, uniform | Traditional |
