## The project

This North Topsail Beach property needed shoreline protection and full water access. NC Marine Construction built a 300 ft vinyl seawall using CMI sheet piling, then a 250 ft dock out to the water. The dock includes a bridge section so boats can still navigate past it.

At the water end, the dock carries an elevator dock lift for the boat and a 1,500 lb PWC swinger lift, plus a WaveArmor drive-on float for a second jet ski.
